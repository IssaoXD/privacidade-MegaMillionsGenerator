2. What the App does

The App generates and organizes number combinations, saves picks and wheels, displays public drawing results and statistics, and provides simulations and number filters. It does not sell lottery tickets, place wagers, accept deposits, transfer money, or award cash prizes. Saved combinations are not lottery tickets or proof of purchase. Strategies, statistics and simulations do not predict results or guarantee winnings.

The App is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Mega Millions, the Mega Millions Consortium, or any participating government lottery. Mega Millions is a trademark of its respective owner.

6. Results and external links

The App retrieves public main-drawing results from NY Open Data. This does not transmit your saved combinations for checking: matching is performed in the App. The results host processes network information under its own policies. See the New York State privacy policy and any notices on the data service.

Links to official lottery information, privacy pages or advertisements open third-party services. Those services handle any information you submit to them under their own policies. This App does not control their content or practices.

7. Retention, deletion and security

Local App information remains until you delete it through available App controls, clear App storage in Android settings, or uninstall the App. To clear storage, use Android Settings → Apps → Mega Millions Generator → Storage → Clear data/storage; labels vary by device. This removes local picks, cached results and reward credits and may reset preferences. The App disables automatic Android backup, but device manufacturers’ transfer or restore behavior can vary.

The developer does not maintain a remote copy of your local picks or credits and cannot restore them after deletion. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to address the request, maintain necessary support records, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. You may request its deletion, subject to applicable exceptions. Google, the results service and other providers apply their own retention practices; clearing local App storage does not delete their records.

The App uses Android’s private application storage for local data. Results requests use HTTPS. Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts user data in transit with TLS. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not email passwords, full card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.
